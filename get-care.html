Good to know

Is it really free?

Yes. Patients never pay us. Some providers and study sponsors pay G44 a referral fee, which is how we keep the service free. Fees never change which options we show you first: fit does.

Who do you work with?

Headache and neurology clinics, licensed online-doctor services, physiotherapy and device providers, and organisations that run approved clinical studies. For injury-related headaches, we can also connect you with independent firms that offer free case reviews.

What do paid studies involve?

Each study is different. Many cover travel and pay for your time. Every study is reviewed by an ethics board, explains the risks in writing first, and lets you leave at any time.

Is my information private?

We use what you send only to answer your request. We never sell it to data brokers. Read our privacy policy for details.

I am under 18.

Ask a parent or guardian to fill in this form with you. Children's headache care is different, and we will look for paediatric options.
