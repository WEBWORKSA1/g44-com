Help millions of people understand their headaches

G44 is a small, remote team. We hire freelancers and part-timers first and grow roles as the site grows. Reader support and partner revenue fund every role.

  • Remote, worldwide
  • Flexible hours
  • Paid per project or per hour

How we hire

  1. Apply with the form below. Two minutes.
  2. Shortlisted people hear from us within two weeks.
  3. A short, paid trial task.
  4. A video call, then an offer.
