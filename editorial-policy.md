## Sources we use

We build every page from the strongest sources available, in this order:

1. **Classification and guidelines:** the International Classification of Headache Disorders, 3rd edition (ICHD-3), and guidance from bodies such as the World Health Organization, the U.S. National Institutes of Health, the UK's NHS and national headache societies.
2. **Peer-reviewed research**, preferring systematic reviews and large studies.
3. **Reputable clinical references** from academic medical centres.

Every condition page lists its sources at the bottom with links.

## How pages are made

Writers draft each page from the sources above. We use software tools, including AI writing and checking tools, to help draft, summarise and proofread; a human editor checks every page against its cited sources before it is published. Pages carry the date they were last reviewed. We review condition pages at least once a year, and sooner when guidance changes.

## Medical review

We are building a panel of clinical reviewers (neurologists, physicians and nurses). As reviewers join, reviewed pages will name the reviewer and their credentials.

## Independence

- Advertisers, sponsors and partners never write, edit or approve health content.
- Sponsored items are labelled "Sponsored" or "Partner".
- Referral fees never change which care options we show first.
- We do not promote unproven cures.

## Tools

Our tools turn published criteria into plain questions. They describe patterns; they do not diagnose. Each tool explains what it is based on.

## Corrections

Spotted an error? Tell us through the [contact page](contact.html) and choose "Correction". We fix confirmed errors promptly and note significant changes on the page.
