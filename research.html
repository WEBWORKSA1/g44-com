Reading research wisely

  • One study is a data point. Guidelines weigh many studies together.
  • Check who was studied. Results in mice, or in a small group, may not apply to you.
  • Look for the type of study. Randomised controlled trials and systematic reviews carry the most weight.
  • Talk before you change. Discuss any new finding with your clinician before changing treatment.

Taking part in a study

Clinical studies test new treatments and better ways to use existing ones. Every approved study is reviewed by an ethics board, explains its risks in writing, and lets you leave at any time. Many cover travel and pay for your time. If you would like help finding one, our matching service is free.
