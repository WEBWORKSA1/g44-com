## Why G44

Almost 3 billion people live with a headache disorder, roughly one person in three, and the share has barely moved since 1990. Most never get a clear name for what they have. G44 exists to change that.

**G44** is the code that the ICD-10, the international classification doctors and insurers use, gives to "other headache syndromes": tension-type, cluster, post-traumatic, medication-overuse, thunderclap, hypnic, cervicogenic and more. We took that code and built the place we wished existed: one plain-English page for every member of the family, honest tools that help you describe your headache, and a free way to reach care.

## What we do

- **Explain.** A guide for every G44 headache and the neighbours they are confused with, written from the international classification (ICHD-3) and peer-reviewed sources listed on each page.
- **Equip.** Free tools that run in your browser: a type finder, a warning-sign checker, a diary, a pressure-change outlook, a rebound-risk check and more.
- **Connect.** A free matching service for specialists, online doctors, drug-free options and recruiting research studies.
- **Update.** Live feeds of new research from PubMed and recruiting studies from ClinicalTrials.gov.

## What we do not do

We do not diagnose, prescribe or replace your doctor. We do not sell personal data. We do not let advertisers or partners write or approve our health content.

## How G44 is paid for

Three ways, all disclosed: display ads served by Google AdSense; referral fees from vetted care providers and study teams when a visitor chooses to be connected; and reader support. Read more in our [editorial policy](editorial-policy.html).
