G44.com ("G44", "we") is operated by {{ site.operator }}. This policy explains how we handle information when you use G44.com.

## What we collect

**Information you send us.** When you use a form (care matching, contact, newsletter, contest entry, job application, pledge or partnership enquiry) we receive what you type, such as your name, email, location and message. The care-matching form may include health details you choose to share, such as your headache type and how often it happens.

**Information stored only on your device.** The headache diary, saved location for the pressure outlook, low-light setting and cookie choice are kept in your browser's local storage. We cannot see them. Clearing your browser data deletes them.

**Information collected automatically.** Like most websites, our host and our advertising partner receive technical data such as your IP address, browser type, pages viewed and approximate location.

## How we use it

- To answer your message or request, and to run contests and hiring.
- With your consent, to send the weekly note.
- For care matching: to find options that fit your request. We share your details with a specific provider or study team only after you approve that option.
- To keep the site secure and working.

We do not sell personal information to data brokers.

## Service providers

- **Form delivery:** forms are delivered by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), which passes your submission to our inbox.
- **Hosting:** GitHub Pages hosts the site.
- **Live data:** the pressure outlook sends the place you choose to Open-Meteo; research feeds call PubMed (U.S. National Library of Medicine) and ClinicalTrials.gov. These calls do not include your name or contact details.
- **Fonts and video:** Google Fonts serves our typeface. YouTube videos load from youtube-nocookie.com only when you press play.

## Advertising and cookies

We use **Google AdSense** to show ads. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services at [policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites), and opt out of personalised advertising at [adssettings.google.com](https://adssettings.google.com). You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies at [aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/).

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google's certified consent message before personalised ads are shown.

## How long we keep it

We keep form submissions for as long as needed to handle your request, then up to 24 months for records, unless the law requires longer. Contest records are kept for 24 months after the contest closes. You can ask us to delete your information sooner.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR and UK GDPR, Canada's PIPEDA and Quebec's Law 25, the California Consumer Privacy Act, or India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or move your information, to withdraw consent, and to object to certain uses. To make a request, use our [contact page](contact.html) and choose "Privacy request". We reply within 30 days.

## Children

G44.com is meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA and UK). Parents and guardians may use the care-matching form on behalf of a child.

## Security

The site is served over HTTPS. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, so please do not send information you consider highly sensitive through our forms.

## International transfers

Our service providers may process information in the United States and other countries. Where required, transfers rely on appropriate safeguards.

## Changes

We will post any changes here and update the date above.
