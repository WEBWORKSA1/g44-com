Official rules

1. Sponsor

The contest is run by G44.com, operated by {{ site.operator }} ("Sponsor"). Questions go through our contact page.

2. No purchase necessary

No purchase, donation or payment of any kind is needed to enter or win. Supporting G44 does not improve your chances.

3. Who can enter

Individuals who are 18 or older, or the age of majority where they live if higher. Void where prohibited or restricted by law. Residents of Quebec, Canada are not eligible. Employees and contractors of the Sponsor and their immediate families may not enter.

4. Entry period

Each contest opens at 00:00 on the first day of the calendar month and closes at 23:59 Eastern Time on the last day of that month.

5. How to enter

Complete the entry form on this page. One entry per person per month. Entries must be your own original work, in English, and on the month's theme. Entries must not make medical claims, promote products or services, or include health details about anyone other than you. Entries that break these rules are disqualified.

6. Judging

A panel of G44 editors scores eligible entries on usefulness to other readers (40%), originality (40%) and clarity (20%). Ties are broken by the usefulness score. The judges' decisions are final.

7. Prizes

One winner receives the first prize and two runners-up each receive the runner-up prize shown on this page. Prizes are delivered as digital gift cards within 30 days of confirmation. Prizes are not transferable. The Sponsor may substitute a prize of equal or greater value.

8. Skill-testing question

Winners resident in Canada must correctly answer a time-limited mathematical skill-testing question before receiving a prize.

9. Winner notification

Winners are emailed within 14 days after the contest closes and must reply within 7 days, or an alternate winner may be chosen. Winners are announced on this page by first name and country.

10. Rights in your entry

You keep the copyright in your entry. By entering, you give G44 a non-exclusive, royalty-free, worldwide licence to publish it, in full or in part, on G44.com, in our newsletter and on our social channels, with your first name and country.

11. Privacy

We use entry details only to run the contest and contact winners, as described in our privacy policy.

12. General

The Sponsor may cancel, suspend or change a contest if fraud, technical failure or anything else outside its control affects its fairness. Winners are responsible for any taxes on prizes. This contest is not sponsored, endorsed or administered by any social media platform.
