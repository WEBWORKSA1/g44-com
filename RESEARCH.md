# G44.com: concept, numbers and benchmark review

Prepared November 2026. Figures are cited where sourced and labelled as estimates where they are our assumptions.

## 1. The pick: a headache hub built on the meaning of "G44"

**G44** is the ICD-10 category for **"other headache syndromes"**: cluster and the other trigeminal autonomic cephalalgias (G44.0), tension-type (G44.2), post-traumatic (G44.3), drug-induced or medication-overuse (G44.4), the complicated syndromes such as new daily persistent and thunderclap headache (G44.5), and hypnic, cough, exertional, stabbing and cervicogenic headache (G44.8). Migraine (G43) is the next-door code.

A 3-character .com is a premium, brandable asset; giving it a real meaning beats an arbitrary brand. The headache niche wins on every axis that matters for a monetized content site:

| Concept considered | Audience size | Ad value | Lead value | Fit with "G44" | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Headache hub (ICD-10 G44)** | ~3 billion people affected | Health display and search | High: specialists, telehealth, clinical studies, injury case reviews | Exact meaning | **Chosen** |
| Firearm content (Glock "G44" model) | Large | Weapons content is restricted for ads | Low | Trademark conflict: GLOCK holds the U.S. "G44" mark (Classes 13, 25) | Rejected |
| Gaming or esports brand | Large | Low CPC | Low | Arbitrary | Rejected |
| Generic finance or grants brand | Medium | High CPC | Medium | Weak, crowded | Rejected |
| Medical-coding-only reference | Narrow | B2B | Low | Strong | Folded in as the code explorer |

## 2. Market numbers

- **~3 billion people** had one or more headache disorders in 2023, about 1 in 3 people; headache disorders rank sixth among causes of health loss (GBD 2023 Headache Collaborators, *The Lancet Neurology*, 2025).
- WHO: headache disorders affect **about 40% of people (3.1 billion in 2021)**; medication-overuse headache affects up to 5% of some populations; episodic tension-type headache is reported by over 70% of some populations; cluster headache affects under 1 in 1,000 adults (WHO fact sheet, October 2025).
- **Medication overuse** drives more than a fifth of all headache-related health loss (GBD 2023), a large, under-served, fixable problem and the reason for our rebound-risk tool.
- Ad market: cross-industry search CPC about $2.96 in Q1 2026; Health and Medical search CPC about $2.62 and display CPC about $0.63 (Ryze 2026 benchmarks). Healthcare search cost per lead about $66 (LocalIQ). Advertisers already pay this much for a headache-care enquiry, which sets the ceiling for our referral pricing.
- Clinical-trial recruitment: per-patient recruitment cost ranges from $143 to $11,392 by therapeutic area (Tufts CSDD), and paid digital recruitment runs $1,500 to $5,000 per enrolled patient. Referral fees of $50 to $300 per qualified, consented lead are realistic.

**The takeaway:** AdSense is the floor; lead generation is the ceiling, by a factor of 4 to 10. The two variables that move the model most are the payout per placed lead and the share of leads you can place. Sign two or three direct partners (a telehealth headache service, a clinical-trial recruitment network, a headache clinic group) before chasing traffic.

**Bold path:** sign a recruitment network and a telehealth partner in the first 90 days, price per qualified lead, and push content for the rare G44 types where competition is thin. **Safe path:** run AdSense plus partner links while the library grows, and add direct deals once traffic proves out.

## 5. Gaps G44 fills

1. **No one joins the code, the plain English, the tools and the care path.** Charities explain; commercial sites monetize; nobody does both with trust.
2. **Rare G44 types are thin everywhere.** Hypnic, NDPH, stabbing, cough and exertional headache have high-intent, low-competition searches.
3. **Medication overuse is the biggest fixable burden** and has almost no consumer tools.
4. **Charity sites rarely capture leads; commercial sites rarely earn trust.** G44 does both with consent and disclosure.

## 6. Risks and how the build handles them

| Risk | Mitigation |
| --- | --- |
| AdSense approval for health (YMYL) content | Full legal set, sources on every page, reviewed dates, no thin pages, labelled ads, custom domain |
| Trademark: GLOCK owns "G44" for firearms and clothing | Different field; disclosure page; no firearm imagery or references beyond the disclaimer |
| Health data in forms | Explicit consent; details shared only after the person approves a specific partner |
| Medical accuracy | ICHD-3-based content, cited sources, editorial policy, medical-reviewer role open |
| Open-Meteo is free for non-commercial use | Endpoint in config; switch to a commercial plan when revenue starts |
| Free hosting limits | Static site on GitHub Pages; all dynamic data fetched in the browser |
