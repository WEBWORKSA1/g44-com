## What we do

- **Low-light mode.** The button at the top dims the whole site for light-sensitive eyes, and remembers your choice.
- **Legible type.** We use Atkinson Hyperlegible, a typeface designed by the Braille Institute to be easy to read for people with low vision.
- **Calm design.** No autoplaying video, no flashing content, and motion is switched off if your device asks for reduced motion.
- **Keyboard and screen readers.** Every tool works with a keyboard, focus is always visible, and the head map has labelled buttons as well as tappable zones.
- **Plain language.** We write short sentences and explain medical terms.

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

## Tell us

If something is hard to use, tell us through the [contact page](contact.html) and choose "Accessibility". Tell us the page and what happened, and we will fix it or send you the information another way.
