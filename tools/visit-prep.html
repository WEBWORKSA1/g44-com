Make a short appointment count

Most headache diagnoses come from the history you give. A clear one-page summary means your doctor spends the visit on decisions, not on reconstructing the story. Bring your diary, every medicine and supplement you take, and the questions that matter most to you. Read our full guide on talking to your doctor about headaches.
