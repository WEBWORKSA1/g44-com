How the outlook works

We take the hourly sea-level air pressure forecast for your location from Open-Meteo and look for the largest change over any 24-hour window in each of the next three days. Falling pressure is weighted more than rising pressure, because falls before fronts and storms are the changes people with headaches report most.

LevelLargest 24-hour swing
LowUnder 3 hPa
Moderate3 to 6 hPa
High6 to 10 hPa
Very high10 hPa or more

What the research says

Weather is one of the most commonly reported headache triggers, especially in migraine. Studies are mixed: some people are clearly sensitive to pressure changes, many are not. The way to find out is to log your headaches in the diary with "Weather change" ticked when it applies, and compare over a few months.
