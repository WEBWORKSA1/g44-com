Why these signs

Most headaches are primary headaches, such as tension-type headache or migraine, and are not dangerous. Doctors use short checklists of warning signs, sometimes remembered as SNOOP, to spot the few headaches that could signal something serious: Systemic symptoms such as fever, Neurological symptoms, sudden Onset, Older age at onset, and changes in Pattern, position or provocation.

This checker sorts those signs into three levels. It cannot examine you. If you are worried, trust that feeling and get checked.
