Why caffeine matters for headaches

Caffeine is a double-edged sword. A modest dose can help some headaches, and it is an ingredient in several headache tablets. But regular high intake makes withdrawal headaches more likely when you miss a dose, and adds to the risk of medication-overuse headache in people who already get frequent headaches.

For most people, steady matters more than the exact number: the same amount at the same times every day, weekends included. If you want to cut back, reduce by about a quarter every few days.
