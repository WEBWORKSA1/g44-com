Why short breaks help

Long, still hours at a screen load the muscles of the neck, shoulders and jaw and tire the eyes, two common contributors to tension-type and neck-related headaches. Short, regular movement breaks are easier to keep up than one long stretch at the end of the day.

The 20-20-20 habit (every 20 minutes, look about 20 feet away for 20 seconds) is widely recommended to ease eye strain. Pair it with the guided neck routine and a sip of water. See our screens and desks guide for setting up your workspace.
