Why slow breathing

Stress tightens the muscles of the scalp, jaw and neck and is one of the most commonly reported headache triggers. Slow, steady breathing, at around six breaths a minute, is one of the simplest ways to calm the body's stress response. Relaxation training and biofeedback, which build on the same idea, have evidence for preventing tension-type headache and migraine.

Use the pacer at the first sign of tension, before bed, or as a daily habit. It complements, but does not replace, medical treatment.
