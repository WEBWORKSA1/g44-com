## What "G44" means here

On G44.com, "G44" refers to the category code for "other headache syndromes" in the International Classification of Diseases, 10th Revision (ICD-10), and its U.S. clinical modification (ICD-10-CM). We use the code descriptively, to identify the medical subject of the site.
