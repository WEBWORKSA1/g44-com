The content, tools and services on G44.com are for **general information and education only**. They are not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, and they do not replace a consultation with a qualified healthcare professional who knows your history.

## In an emergency

Call your local emergency number (911 in the US and Canada, 999 in the UK, 112 in the EU and India) for:

- a sudden, severe headache that peaks within about a minute;
- a headache with weakness, numbness, confusion, trouble speaking, fainting, a seizure or loss of vision;
- a headache with fever and a stiff neck, or a rash that does not fade under pressure;
- a headache after a head injury, especially with vomiting or drowsiness.

## Tools are not diagnoses

The type finder, warning-sign checker, rebound-risk check and other tools turn published criteria into plain questions. Their results describe how your answers match common patterns. Only a clinician who assesses you can make a diagnosis.

## Medicines

Never start, stop or change a medicine because of something you read here. Stopping some medicines suddenly can be dangerous. Talk to your doctor or pharmacist first.

## Care matching and studies

When we connect you with a provider or a research study, that provider or study team is responsible for your care and for checking whether a study is right for you. G44.com does not provide medical services.

## Weather and research feeds

The pressure outlook uses third-party weather data and is not a medical forecast. Research feeds list new publications and studies; inclusion is not an endorsement.

## No relationship

Using G44.com does not create a doctor-patient or nurse-patient relationship with anyone.
