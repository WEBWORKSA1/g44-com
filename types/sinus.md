True sinus headache comes from **rhinosinusitis**, inflammation or infection of the air spaces around the nose. It is real, but it is far less common than people think. In one large study, close to nine in ten people who believed they had "sinus headaches" actually met the criteria for [migraine](types/migraine.html).

## Signs it really is your sinuses

- It follows a cold, with a **blocked nose** and **thick, discoloured mucus**.
- **Face pain or pressure** that is worse when you bend forward.
- A **reduced sense of smell**, and sometimes a **fever**.
- It improves as the infection clears, usually within two to three weeks.

## Signs it is probably migraine

- The "sinus" headaches come back again and again, with no infection.
- They throb, make you feel sick, or make light and noise hard to bear.
- Decongestants and antibiotics never seem to help.
- They are linked to triggers such as sleep loss, stress, periods or weather.

Migraine can cause a blocked or runny nose and watery eyes on its own, which is why the confusion is so common.

## Self-care for a sinus infection

Rest, fluids, pain relievers, saline nasal rinses and warm compresses help most people. Most sinus infections are caused by viruses and clear without antibiotics.

## See a doctor if

Symptoms last more than about 10 days, get worse after starting to improve, or keep returning. Get **urgent** help for swelling or redness around the eyes, double vision, a severe headache, confusion or a stiff neck.
