Our rules for partners

  • Every sponsored item is labelled as sponsored or as a partner link.
  • Partners never write, edit or approve our health content.
  • We do not promote unproven cures, prescription-drug offers that break local rules, or anything that discourages people from seeing a doctor.
  • Patient details go only to the partners a patient has approved.
  • Display ads are served by Google AdSense under Google's own policies.
