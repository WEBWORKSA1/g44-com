Questions people ask first

What does G44 mean?

G44 is a category in the ICD-10, the international system doctors and insurers use to code diagnoses. It covers "other headache syndromes": tension-type, cluster, post-traumatic, medication-overuse, thunderclap, hypnic, cervicogenic and more. Migraine has its own category, G43.

Can this site diagnose my headache?

No. Our tools help you describe your headache clearly and spot patterns, which makes your doctor's job easier. Only a clinician who examines you can diagnose you.

Is G44 free?

Yes. Ads, partners and reader support pay for the site, so every guide and tool is free. Care matching is free for patients.

Where is my diary stored?

In your own browser, on your own device. We never see it unless you choose to export it and send it somewhere.

Know the warning signs

Most headaches are not dangerous. A few need help fast. Our one-minute checker tells you whether to call for help now, see a doctor today, or book a routine visit.
